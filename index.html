How can circles draw a face?

Your photo becomes a local sketch. Contours are sampled evenly and a discrete Fourier transform turns them into rotating circles. The top chain gives x(t); the left gives y(t). Their projections meet at the pen. Automatic N preserves detail, and the pen lifts between strokes.

Default links include coefficients, pen lifts and coarse sketch tones. If you opt in to a full sketch, the link also holds its decryption key. Original photos are never uploaded.

Original photos stay on your device. Full sketches are uploaded only with your opt-in, end-to-end encrypted, and stored for 30 days.

Links include formula and coarse tones; opting in adds the full sketch's decryption key. Anyone with the link can view, save and forward the portrait.
