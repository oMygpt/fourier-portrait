How can circles draw a face?

Lines are 1D Fourier: rotating circles draw the strokes. Tones are 2D Fourier: the sketch comes from the U²-Net neural network; we split it into plane waves and add them back from low to high frequency — broad shading first, then hair and wrinkles. With every frequency added, it equals the sketch exactly.

Your photo becomes a local sketch. Contours are sampled evenly and a discrete Fourier transform turns them into rotating circles. The top chain gives x(t); the left gives y(t). Their projections meet at the pen. Automatic N preserves detail, and the pen lifts between strokes.

HD sharing (default) encrypts the sketch and full formula locally before upload; the QR holds only a short pointer and the key. The copied text link also carries coefficients, pen lifts and coarse tones, so it still draws after expiry. Original photos are never uploaded.

Original photos stay on your device. When you make a card, the sketch and formula are uploaded end-to-end encrypted by default, kept 30 days, and can be withdrawn any time under “My shares” — or unchecked.

QR codes and links hold the decryption key; anyone with them can view, save and forward the portrait.
